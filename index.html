1. Reading

When Work Does Not Stop

Maria has been working as a project manager at a tech company for three years. Lately, her team has been dealing with overlapping deadlines, and the pressure has been building steadily. She has been staying late most evenings, and her sleep has been suffering as a result.

To cope, Maria has been experimenting with different strategies. She has been blocking time in her calendar for focused work and turning off notifications during those windows. She has also been meeting with her team every morning to identify obstacles early, which has been helping them avoid last-minute crises.

One thing that has made a difference is how Maria has been communicating with her manager. Rather than waiting until a problem becomes serious, she has been raising concerns early. Her manager has been responding well, and together they have been redistributing tasks more fairly.

Maria admits the pressure has not disappeared entirely. But having a routine, setting clear limits on her working hours, and asking for support when needed have been her most effective tools so far.
